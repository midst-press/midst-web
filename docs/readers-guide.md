# How to watch a poem

Every poem on Midst is a timelapse. You can read the finished poem, and you can watch it being written: every word typed, moved and taken out, in the order the poet did it.

When a poem opens, you see it finished. The controls under the poem let you go back and watch.

## Play and pause

Press **Play** (the triangle) to watch the poem being written from the beginning. The page follows the place where the poet is writing.

Press **Pause** (the two bars) to stop where you are. Press **Play** again to carry on from there.

When the timelapse reaches the end it stops on the finished poem. **Play** then starts again from the beginning.

## The timeline

The line beside the buttons is the **timeline**. Its left end is the empty page and its right end is the finished poem. The dot shows where you are.

Drag the dot, or click anywhere on the line, to go to that moment. The poem pauses there.

## Draft markers

Some poets mark moments in their writing: the end of a first draft, a version they sent to a friend. Each one is a **draft marker**, and it appears as a small label above the timeline, pointing at its moment.

The words on a label are the poet's own name for that draft. If the poet did not name it, the label says Draft 1, Draft 2 and so on. A long name is cut short; rest your pointer on the label to read all of it.

Click a draft marker to go to that moment. The poem pauses there.

A poem with no labels above its timeline has no draft markers.

## The date and time under the timeline

Under the timeline, a date and time move along with the dot. They show when the poet made the change you are looking at, so you can see whether a poem took an evening or a year.

## Skip to the start or the end

**Skip to start** takes you to the empty page. **Skip to end** takes you to the finished poem. The poem pauses in both cases.

## Speed

Press **Playback speed** (the dial) to open a slider. Move it up to watch faster and down to watch slower. The number beside it is the speed in frames per second (fps): how many of the poet's changes you see each second, from 1 to 60.

Changing the speed does not pause the poem or move you along the timeline. Press the dial again, or click anywhere else, to close the slider.

## Using the keyboard

Press Space to play or pause.

Press Tab to move between the controls. When a control is selected, Space or Enter presses it.

When the timeline is selected, the arrow keys move one change at a time.

When the speed slider is open, the arrow keys change the speed and Escape closes it.
