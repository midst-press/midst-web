Midst is currently **closed** to submissions.

Please note that we *only* consider .midst files—poems written and edited with [the Midst app](/app).
We do not accept poems in any other format made with any other software (.doc, .docx, .pdf, .txt, etc.).

The app is free to download and free to use, built by and for writers. Everything you write with it is completely
private and belongs 100% to you. It works on Mac, Windows, and Linux. [Download it here](/app).

[Sign up to our newsletter](#newsletter) to find out about open submission windows and other opportunities.
